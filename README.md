# HA-60 Personality Inventory

An unofficial, original-item **analog of the HEXACO model of personality** — a
single self-contained HTML file for **personal, educational use**.

## Quick start

Download [`hexaco-analog.html`](hexaco-analog.html) and open it in any modern
browser (double-clicking the file works — no server, install, or internet
connection needed). Answer the 60 statements (~10 minutes), then view your
results and click **Download report card (PNG)** to export a shareable graphic
of your profile.

Answers are saved to your browser's local storage as you go, so you can close
the tab and resume later. Nothing ever leaves your device.

## What it measures

Sixty original statements rated on a 5-point agree/disagree scale, covering the
six HEXACO factors (10 items each) and all 24 published facets (2–3 items each):

| Factor | Facets |
|---|---|
| **H** — Honesty-Humility | Sincerity, Fairness, Greed Avoidance, Modesty |
| **E** — Emotionality | Fearfulness, Anxiety, Dependence, Sentimentality |
| **X** — eXtraversion | Social Self-Esteem, Social Boldness, Sociability, Liveliness |
| **A** — Agreeableness | Forgivingness, Gentleness, Flexibility, Patience |
| **C** — Conscientiousness | Organization, Diligence, Perfectionism, Prudence |
| **O** — Openness to Experience | Aesthetic Appreciation, Inquisitiveness, Creativity, Unconventionality |

Items are presented interleaved across factors, and roughly half are
reverse-keyed (scored as `6 − response`). A factor score is the mean of its 10
items; a facet score is the mean of its 2–3 items — all on the raw 1–5 scale.
Descriptive bands are relative to the scale midpoint of 3: below 2.5 = lower
range, 2.5–3.5 = mid-range, above 3.5 = higher range.

## Results and export

- On-screen: a six-axis radar chart of factor scores, factor bars with band
  labels, per-factor interpretation text, and all 24 facet bars.
- Export: a **full report card PNG** (2400×2760) combining the radar chart,
  factor and facet scores, and interpretations — rendered locally on a canvas,
  always in the light theme for consistent output.

## Important disclaimer

This is **not** the HEXACO-PI-R. The HEXACO-PI-R is a copyrighted instrument by
Kibeom Lee and Michael C. Ashton (see [hexaco.org](https://hexaco.org)). Every
item here is original text written to cover the same factor/facet structure
described in the published HEXACO literature.

This tool has **no established reliability, validity, or population norms** —
scores are raw item averages. Treat the results as a structured
self-reflection exercise, not a validated psychological assessment. Not for
research, clinical, hiring, or any decision-making use. If you want the real
instrument, request it from the authors at hexaco.org.
